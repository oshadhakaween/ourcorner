I. the seed

Every story starts small — a seed, a hello, a first spark I didn’t know would grow into this.

II. the reach

Then it kept reaching for you, quietly, the way a stem reaches for light without needing to be told.

III. the waiting

Somewhere between then and now, it became something I couldn’t stop noticing — soft, closed, about to open.

IV. the opening

Petal by petal, it stopped holding back. That’s what you did to me, too.

V. the bloom

Wide open now. Nothing left folded away.

VI. the meaning

This is what it looks like when something is loved into growing. This is what you did.
